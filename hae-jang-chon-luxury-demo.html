The Koreatown classic, sizzling since 2002

Hae Jang Chon set the standard for all-you-can-eat Korean BBQ in Los Angeles. The Los Angeles Times has called it one of the most delicious Korean dining deals in Southern California — and more than two decades later, the line out the door on 6th Street says the same thing.

The stone grill

A 2-inch thick stone grill made of living rock from Korea. It brings out the flavor of the meat, keeps it tender, and releases healthy minerals as it heats.

They grill for you

Servers cook everything at your table — cutting, flipping and plating — so you can concentrate on eating. First time? They will walk you through the whole spread.

Open late

Until midnight most nights and 2 AM on Fridays and Saturdays. One of Koreatown's most beloved late-night destinations.

The Experience

How a night at Hae Jang Chon works

Come hungry. Everything below is how the restaurant actually runs it — from the stone grill to the kimchi fried rice finale.

1 · Sit down to banchan

Your table arrives loaded: kimchi, spinach, bean sprouts, fish cake, pickled radish and more — refillable, all night. Your server explains the menu and the sauces: sweet soy, doenjang (bean paste), gochujang (chili paste) and sesame oil.

2 · Order up to 4 at a time

Pick up to four grill items per round — brisket, galbi, bulgogi, pork belly, tongue, seafood. Your server grills it all on the 2-inch stone grill, cuts it, and plates it so nothing overcooks.

3 · Finish with fried rice

The finale: kimchi fried rice cooked right on the grill at the end of the meal — add cheese on top if you like. Steamed egg and corn cheese run through the meal as sides.

The stone grill

“One of the best features of our restaurant is the stone grill. 2-inch thick stone grill is made of living rock from Korea. The stone grill not only brings out the exquisite taste, it also keeps the meat tender for a long period of time.” — Hae Jang Chon

Canadian pork, USDA Choice beef

“We strictly use Canadian Pork for our menu. Canadian Pork has proportionately balanced fat and meat.” The beef is USDA Choice or higher, including prime brisket, prime chuck short rib and prime steak.

Good to know: expect a wait at peak dinner hours — 40 minutes to 2 hours on busy nights is normal, and late night (after midnight on weekends) is often the fastest way in. The dining room is warm wood with a traditional Korean atmosphere, and the restaurant is wheelchair accessible, family-friendly and good for groups.

Good to know

Is everything really all-you-can-eat? ＋

Yes — one price per person covers the full AYCE menu: beef, pork, chicken, seafood, banchan and sides. Lunch Special $29.99 (11 AM–3 PM), Regular Lunch $37.99 (11 AM–3 PM), Dinner $43.99 (3 PM–close), as listed on the restaurant's menu.

How does ordering work? ＋

Order up to 3–4 grill items at a time and your server cooks them for you at the table, cutting and plating as they go. Order more rounds as you like. Unfinished meat may incur an extra charge, so order what you can finish.

Do I need a reservation? ＋

Reservations are taken for parties of 6 or more. Smaller parties are first-come, first-served. At peak dinner times the wait is often 40 minutes to 2 hours; coming early, off-peak, or late night usually means a shorter wait.

Is there parking? ＋

Yes — valet parking is available in the shared lot, and there is street parking nearby. The restaurant is wheelchair accessible.

What about takeout or delivery? ＋

Takeout and delivery are available, including à la carte dishes like bulgogi, beef brisket, beef tongue and pork belly with rice and side dishes. The all-you-can-eat experience is dine-in only.

What drinks do they serve? ＋

Beer and wine, including soju (Chamisul and pomegranate soju) and Terra beer, plus soft drinks. Guests often pair the grill with soju or beer.
